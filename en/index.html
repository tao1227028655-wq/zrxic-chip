Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about sourcing electronic components from Shenzhen and Hong Kong.

How long does it take to get a BOM quotation?

Standard response time is within 24 hours. For urgent requests, we can respond within 2-4 hours during Shenzhen business hours.

Do you supply to Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan?

Yes. We regularly ship to Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and other CIS countries. We understand the logistics and payment landscape.

How do you guarantee authentic components?

All components are sourced from manufacturers and authorized distributors. We provide lot traceability, pre-shipment photos of markings, and offer lab verification (X-ray, decapsulation) on request. 100% refund if counterfeit is detected.

What brands do you supply?

We supply 26+ brands: Texas Instruments, Analog Devices, STMicroelectronics, Microchip, Infineon, NXP, ON Semi, Maxim, Xilinx/AMD, Intel/Altera, Vishay, Murata, TE, Molex, TDK, Renesas, and more. We also offer Chinese alternatives (GigaDevice, Artery, SGMicro).

What payment methods do you accept?

CNY, RUB, and USD via bank transfer. We work with each client to find the most suitable payment method for their situation.

Do you offer Chinese alternative components?

Yes. We offer pin-to-pin compatible Chinese alternatives: GigaDevice (replaces STM32), Artery (replaces ST), SGMicro (replaces ADI/TI op-amps), XLSEMI (replaces TI DC-DC), Huajing (replaces Infineon MOSFET). Free compatibility verification included.
